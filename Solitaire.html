Solitaire Suite Rules

1. Classic Solitaire (Klondike)

Build all 4 Foundation piles up in suit from Ace to King. On the 7 tableau columns, stack cards downward in alternating colors (e.g., Red 7 on Black 8). Only a King can fill an empty column.

2. Pyramid Solitaire

Clear the entire 28-card pyramid by pairing any two uncovered cards that add up to 13. Values: Ace = 1, 2–10 = face value, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, and Kings = 13 (tap a King by itself to remove it immediately!). Match pyramid cards with the Stock or Waste pile too.

3. TriPeaks Solitaire

Clear all three peaks by tapping uncovered cards that are 1 rank higher or lower than the current Waste pile card, regardless of suit. Ranks wrap around seamlessly (King ↔ Ace ↔ 2). Build long streaks before drawing from the Stock for bonus points!

4. Spider Solitaire (1, 2, or 4 Suits)

Build complete descending sequences from King down to Ace of the exact same suit across the 10 columns; completed runs automatically fly off to the Foundation! You can place any card on a card 1 rank higher, but you can only move a group of cards together if they share the same suit. Tap the Stock to deal 10 new cards.
