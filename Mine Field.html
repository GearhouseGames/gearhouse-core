HOW TO PLAY MINESWEEPER

Guaranteed Safe Start

Your first dig is always 100% safe and opens a clear starting zone. Each number tells you how many mines touch that square (including diagonals).

PC & Laptop Controls

Left-Click to dig a tile. Right-Click (or Shift+Click) to cycle Flag 🚩 / Question ❓. Click a revealed number once its surrounding flags match its value to fast-clear ("Chord") all neighbors!

Mobile & Touch Controls

Use the bottom DIG / FLAG toggle for rapid 1-tap play, or Hold your finger briefly on any tile to plant a flag instantly. Use the Zoom (- / FIT / +) buttons to scale big boards.
